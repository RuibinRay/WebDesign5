          The name

          The opposite of an apocalypse.

          kalyptein
          Greek. To cover.
          apo·kalypsis
          An un-covering. The end of the world, more or less.
          calyptratus
          The veiled chameleon, named for the casque on its head: a hood it is born with. That is Hex.
          Kalypt
          A hood you choose.
