Brand breakdown

Kalypt, taken apart.

A concept hoodie brand in pixels. One cut, eight palettes, and a chameleon named Hex who walks the site with you. This page says what every part is and why it is there. Everything pixel is drawn by the page; the photographs and two films are the only things left to make.

The one idea

Palette swap. In old games, a new character was often the same sprite in new colours. Kalypt is one cut of hoodie, recoloured five times. Every part of the site does the same trick: the hoodies, the mascot, the city's signs and the page's own accent colour all read from one palette and swap together.

The second rule: scrolling is the controller. Nothing plays on a timer except rain and stars. Scroll to walk Hex, to slide the hoodies, to turn the cut round.

The name

Kalypt comes from the Greek kalyptein, to cover. An apocalypse (apo-kalypsis) is an un-covering. A hood is the opposite. The mascot is a veiled chameleon, Chamaeleo calyptratus: the species name comes from kalyptra, a veil or cover, for the casque on its head. A hooded animal that swaps palettes.

A web search on 2026-11-20 found no hoodie or streetwear brand called Kalypt. That is a quick check, not a trademark clearance.

The Eye (a chameleon's turret eye, 7 × 7 px) and the letters (7 × 9 px each, drawn for this brand, not a font). The lower half of the letters dithers into the accent colour the way the hoodies do.

The logo

The brand logo is Hex in his roundel: the mascot's head and hood, cut at the chest by a pixel ring. It is drawn from his real sprite, so the logo and the mascot can never drift apart. It is for the web and everything around the clothes (the site's nav and tab icon, social cards, hang tags, packaging, stickers), not a print on the garments. On the clothes the brand stays quiet: only the small Eye.

The mascot: Hex

Hex is a veiled chameleon, 48 × 51 px, named for hex colour codes. He is not a set of drawings. He is built from parts every time he is needed (tail spiral, two legs, body, head with casque, turret eye, arm, and the hoodie's body, hood, sleeve, pocket and cord), so any pose is one call and any palette is free.

The range

Each palette is two colours (upper and fade), a glow for the piping, and the colours the page takes when that hoodie is on screen. Each is named for something a city does after dark.

The fade is ordered dither (a 4 × 4 Bayer grid) in four palettes and noise in Static. On the real garment that is a jacquard knit, not a print.

Type and colour

Pixelify Sans 700

Anything that shouts: headings, prices, buttons, numbers. Always uppercase.

Martian Mono, width 87.5

Everything else: body copy, labels, the HUD. A monospace built on a grid, so it sits next to pixels without pretending to be them.

Page colours: void #07060D, panel #100E1C, line #2A2644, text #EDEBFF, mute #9A95C4. The accent is never fixed: it is whichever palette is on screen.
