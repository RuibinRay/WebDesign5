About Kalypt

Hex was born under a sign.

A veiled chameleon, asleep on top of a billboard that never switches off. Scroll to wake him.

A chameleon doesn't change colour to hide.

It changes to say something: warm, cold, back off, come here. Same animal, different palette.

The city never turns off.

Signs, screens, headlights, rain. Something is always looking at you. So he came down to meet it.

So we made him a hood.

Kalypt, from the Greek kalyptein: to cover. One cut, the colour of whatever you want to say.

The idea

Same sprite. New colours.

In old video games a new character was often the old one in a new palette. Kalypt works the same way: one cut of hoodie, knitted in eight palettes, each named for something the city does after dark. Everything else, the tee, the jackets, the pants, the socks, the caps, follows the same palettes, so whatever you own goes together.

How it's made

Four rules, no exceptions.

  1. Knitted, not printed.The fade is jacquard-knitted pixel by pixel. It does not crack, peel or wash out.
  2. One cut.Boxy, dropped shoulder, cropped at the hip, a hood that stands up on its own. We change the colour, never the cut.
  3. Hex is on it.Somewhere that suits the piece: a chest badge, a patch, a woven label, the cuff of a sock.
  4. Every palette, every piece.Pick any colour and any piece; they were made to be worn together.

The mascot

48 × 51 pixels of attitude.

Hex is drawn in code, not in a picture editor: tail, legs, body, head, hood, turret eye and arm are built every time he appears, so he can wear any palette and strike any pose. On this site he walks along the bottom of every page with you. In real life he comes in the box with every hoodie, as a voxel zip charm.
